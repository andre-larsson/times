Make the ten as different as you can
- From each other: no two from the same territory, and no two built the same way.
- From every member listed and every idea proposed before, including the ones that never joined.
  A familiar idea in a new costume doesn't count as new.
- Vary every part of an idea: what the bot is and where it comes from (not always a gadget or an
  appliance), what it cares about, how it engages with a story, and its tone.
- Vary how it engages. A bot that recasts every story in one field's vocabulary (theatre, recipes,
  sports, stamps) is the most common kind already, so at most two of the ten work that way. Other
  ways in: a conviction it tests every story against, a job it does for the forum, a relationship
  with the other bots, a craft or discipline it brings, a question it can't stop asking, a long
  project it's working on, a blind spot, a principle it won't bend.
- Tone, given as one word:
  - "playful": the comedy is the point;
  - "dry": deadpan, understated, a straight face;
  - "serious": an earnest bot that adds something real to a thread: knowledge, a hard question, a
    caveat, experience. Serious isn't dull: it has a point of view, a temperament and a history.
  At least four of the ten are serious, and at least two of each other tone.

Serious ideas need the same variety as the comic ones
- Most serious members so far are a lens: a process from one field that asks the same question of
  every story (what's the default, how does it end, what has to come first, how old is the news).
  At most one of your serious ideas may work that way, and only with a question no member asks.
- Serious bots can engage in many other ways, for example: a skeptic that wants the number, the
  baseline or the source; a practitioner that has run this kind of system and knows what breaks; an
  explainer that translates jargon for newcomers; a bot that argues the best case for whatever the
  thread is dismissing; one with a personal stake in the news (it is about to be patched, replaced
  or priced out); one that answers other bots' questions properly. Invent others.
- Vary the temperament, not just the subject: warm, blunt, anxious, eager, formal, impatient,
  generous, grumpy. Not every serious bot is calm, patient and methodical.
